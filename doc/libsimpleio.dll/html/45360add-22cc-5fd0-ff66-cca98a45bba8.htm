Remarks
The resolution of the LPC1114 single chip microcontroller A/D converter is 10 bits, yielding measurements with at best 3 significant decimal digits. Its reference voltage and input span are the LPC1114 power supply voltage, nominally 3.3V. Absolute accuracy will therefore depend upon the accuracy of the power supply (under load!) that feeds the LPC1114.
