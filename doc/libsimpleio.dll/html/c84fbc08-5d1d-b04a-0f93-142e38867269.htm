Remarks
The M5 Stack Ultrasonic-I2C module pulls SCL low (i.e.I2C slave clock stretch) for 50 milliseconds after accepting the ping command. On Raspberry Pi's 1 to 4, which do not support I2C slave clock stretch, we have to wait it out with a safe margin before reading the echo response bytes. The RP1 I/O Controller on Raspberry Pi 5 boards does support clock stretching.
